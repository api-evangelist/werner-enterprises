A driver’s first year on the road is an important milestone; at Werner, it is designed to be the launchpad for career growth rather than a temporary stop. More than 60% of our fleet consists of experienced drivers with at least one year of safe driving under their belts who choose to stay and grow their careers here. Whether you are just starting out or have thousands of miles behind you, Werner is built to be a long-term destination.
