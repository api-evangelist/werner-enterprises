Today’s furniture customers can easily compare hundreds of products online, read thousands of reviews and have a sofa delivered within days. But while shopping has largely shifted to digital, one thing hasn’t changed: the delivery experience remains the primary face-to-face interaction a customer has with your brand. That single interaction carries enormous weight for customer perception.
