Originally posted on Supply Chain Dive In today’s fast-paced logistics landscape, having the right mix of shipping solutions can greatly improve efficiency, reduce costs and support sustainability goals. Intermodal shipping—the integration of multiple transportation methods such as truck and rail—is quickly gaining traction as an important business solution for companies. In fact, according to the Intermodal Association of North America (IANA), total intermodal shipping volume rose 9.8% year-over-year in Q3 of 2024 – the fourth consecutive quarter of YoY growth.
