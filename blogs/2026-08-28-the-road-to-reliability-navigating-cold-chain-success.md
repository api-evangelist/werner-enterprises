The pharmaceutical supply chain is one of the most complex and highly regulated in the world for a reason. Transporting medications across long distances demands precision, control and accountability. Successful transport and delivery depend heavily on maintaining strict temperature integrity, complying with stringent government and industry requirements and using the right equipment and trained personnel.
