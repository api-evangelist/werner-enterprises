Originally posted on Trucking Dive Werner Enterprises’ technological advances are fueling its logistics growth, Chris Neil, SVP of pricing and strategic planning, said at the Deutsche Bank’s 2025 Transportation Conference on Aug. 12. Neil said Werner is working on building out its transportation management system platform, branded as EDGE TMS, to create a single platform with all of its loads visible on that system.
