Originally posted on Food Logistics In today’s fast-paced logistics landscape, having the right mix of shipping solutions is vital to moving dry food and cold chain freight quickly and efficiently. Intermodal shipping is becoming increasingly popular in the food and beverage industry as a method to improve efficiency, reduce costs and support larger sustainability goals. Intermodal shipping is the integration of multiple transportation methods, such as truck and rail.
