Originally posted on Journal of Commerce The supply chain has become a frontline issue for industries that can’t afford disruptions. In healthcare, where medication viability depends on precise temperature thresholds, and in high-end technology, where a single lost server can upend critical infrastructure, freight isn’t just freight—it’s a matter of performance, protection and public trust. As global regulations tighten and customer expectations accelerate, the pressure is on.
